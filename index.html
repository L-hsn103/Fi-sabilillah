Our Origin & Philosophy

Ending the Dilemma of Wealth-Based Charity

Article 4 of the Fi Sabilillah Constitution establishes why this movement was born and how we challenge conventional, status-driven philanthropy.

Vision

A society where every individual lives with dignity, self-reliance, and equal opportunity—free from wealth-based social hierarchies.

Mission

To empower economically disadvantaged individuals to become self-reliant, self-respecting citizens through discreet, sustainable support and ethical giving practices.

Article 6: Operational Priorities

Three Pillars of Targeted Action

Our constitution mandates that all activities and expenditures strictly follow three prioritized programs.

1

Sustainable Self-Reliance

Providing production tools such as sewing machines, micro-trade seed supplies, vocational instruments, and business mentoring.

Learn more →
2

Ethical Giving & Human Dignity

Campaigning against ostentatious photo-op donations. Educating society on confidential, respectful assistance.

Learn more →
3

Disaster & Accident Relief

Rapid, empathetic assistance to communities struck by sudden natural disasters or individual catastrophic accidents.
