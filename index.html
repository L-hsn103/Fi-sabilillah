Our Origin & Philosophy

Ending the Dilemma of Wealth-Based Charity

Article 4 of the Fi Sabilillah Constitution establishes why this movement was born and how we challenge conventional, status-driven philanthropy.

The Conventional Charity Pitfall

"A notable drawback in modern society's customary donation culture is public giving driven by the donor's pursuit of social prestige and acclaim, which inevitably humiliates and degrades the recipient's personal dignity."

Furthermore, routine temporary handouts create recurring dependency without offering a realistic path toward long-term financial self-reliance.

Constitution Chapter 2, Article 4 (Vision & Mission)

The Fi Sabilillah Vision: Dignity First

"A group of conscious students established 'Fi Sabilillah' to dismantle wealth-based social stratification and provide sustainable, discreet support while keeping the recipient's dignity untarnished."

Rather than perpetuating aid dependency, we prioritize lasting self-reliance—empowering recipients with skills and tools to emerge as proud, productive citizens contributing to society.

Sole Goal: Seeking the Pleasure of Allah (আল্লাহর সন্তুষ্টি)
Article 5: Supreme Goal & Objective
“আল্লাহর সন্তুষ্টি অর্জনের লক্ষ্যে অর্থনৈতিকভাবে পিছিয়ে পড়া মানুষদের আত্মনির্ভরশীল ও আত্মমর্যাদাসম্পন্ন নাগরিক হিসেবে গড়ে তুলতে সহায়তা করা এবং মানব-মর্যাদার প্রকৃত অর্থ প্রতিষ্ঠা করা।”

"To assist economically disadvantaged individuals in becoming self-reliant and self-respecting citizens solely for the pleasure of Allah, and to re-establish the true essence of human dignity."

Article 6: Operational Priorities

Three Pillars of Targeted Action

Our constitution mandates that all activities and expenditures strictly follow three prioritized programs.

1 Top Priority

Sustainable Self-Reliance

অর্থনৈতিকভাবে পিছিয়ে পড়া মানুষদের আত্মনির্ভরশীল ও আত্মমর্যাদাসম্পন্ন হিসেবে গড়ে তুলতে সহায়তা

Providing production tools (such as sewing machines, micro-trade seed supplies, vocational instruments, and business mentoring) so families generate their own livelihoods rather than relying on cyclic relief.

Focus: Vocational Independence Priority #1
2 Core Awareness

Ethical Giving & Human Dignity

সমাজে সঠিক দান পদ্ধতি ও মানব মর্যাদার গুরুত্ব সম্পর্কে জনসচেতনতা সৃষ্টি

Campaigning against ostentatious photo-op donations. Educating society and donors on confidential, respectful assistance that safeguards the self-esteem of beneficiaries.

Focus: Social Paradigm Shift Priority #2
3 Emergency Standby

Disaster & Accident Relief

প্রাকৃতিক দুর্যোগ বা দুর্ঘটনায় ক্ষতিগ্রস্ত ব্যক্তিদের সহায়তা প্রদান

Rapid, empathetic assistance to communities struck by sudden natural disasters (floods, cyclones) or individual catastrophic accidents, handled with utmost confidentiality.

Focus: Crisis Humanitarian Aid Priority #3

Article 12: Unique Democratic Framework

Annual April Democratic Elections

Every member is both a candidate and a voter. No candidate campaigns for themselves.

Scheduled: First Week of April (Annual)
Universal Participation

All active members are candidates and voters. No separate candidacy filings.

Zero Self-Voting

No member can cast a vote for themselves, ensuring selfless evaluation based on merit.

Sequential Transition

President & GS are elected first; they then collaboratively appoint remaining officers.

Emergency Recall

If 3 members petition for re-election, an emergency vote must be immediately called.
