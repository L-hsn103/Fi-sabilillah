Chapter 6: Fund Ethics & Auditing

Transparency

Articles 13 and 14 establish a strict fiduciary framework to ensure every single Taka directly serves humanitarian empowerment.

Funding Sources

Members contribute a minimum of 50 BDT per month, providing predictable, independent baseline funding.

Third-Party Donations
Via Verified Members

Donations from non-members are received strictly through verified members of the organization.

Fund Management Policy

Fund Management Policy

All expenditures strictly align with the three constitutional programs and require prior approval in formal meetings.

Expenditure Approval

No expenditure is made without formal executive consensus. The Treasurer leads the full end-of-year audit.

Auditing

At the conclusion of each fiscal year, an exhaustive financial audit is conducted, spearheaded by the Treasurer (Article 14).

Financial Accountability

Fi Sabilillah does not process public commercial payments. External donations must be coordinated through an active member or official email.
