Chapter 3: Member Qualifications

Membership

Articles 7 and 8 outline the qualifications and responsibilities of membership. We welcome students and citizens dedicated to sustainable community empowerment.

Eligibility Requirements

Eligibility Requirements

16+

Minimum Age 16 Years

Applicant must be at least 16 years of age.

Zero Membership Application Fee

No application fee whatsoever is required (Article 7, Clause 3).

Recommendation Required

Must be formally recommended by an active member.

Active Commitment

Demonstrated interest and promise of active voluntary participation.

Member Responsibilities

Member Responsibilities

Attend scheduled monthly meetings.

Contribute minimum 50 BDT monthly (Article 13).

Uphold the Constitution of Fi Sabilillah.

Participate in voluntary activities.

Application Process

Application Process

1

Complete the application form below.

Fill in all required fields accurately.

2

Secure a recommendation from an active member.

Provide the name of your recommending member.

3

Executive Council reviews your application.

Your referral will be verified by the council.

4

Receive confirmation of membership status.

You will be notified of the decision.
