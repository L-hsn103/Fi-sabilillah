About Fi Sabilillah

Established on April 10, 2021, Fi Sabilillah is a non-political, non-profit, and voluntary organization dedicated to restoring human dignity.

Establishment

Established

April 10, 2021

Fi Sabilillah was founded by a group of conscious students in Bangladesh on April 10, 2021. The organization was established to dismantle wealth-based social stratification and provide sustainable, discreet support while keeping the recipient's dignity untarnished.

Who We Are

Who We Are

Fi Sabilillah is a non-political, non-profit, and voluntary organization founded in Bangladesh by a group of conscious students committed to restoring human dignity through sustainable self-reliance and ethical giving.

Vision

A society where every individual lives with dignity, self-reliance, and equal opportunity—free from wealth-based social hierarchies.

Mission

To empower economically disadvantaged individuals to become self-reliant, self-respecting citizens through discreet, sustainable support and ethical giving practices.

Core Values

Core Values

Human Dignity

Every person deserves respect and dignity, regardless of economic status.

Self-Reliance

We prioritize lasting empowerment over temporary handouts.

Ethical Giving

Assistance is given confidentially, without publicity or prestige-seeking.

Voluntary Spirit

Our work is driven by voluntary service, seeking the pleasure of Allah.

Objectives

Objectives

Eliminate wealth-based social stratification.

Promote sustainable self-reliance through vocational support.

Advocate ethical, dignity-preserving donation practices.

Provide rapid, confidential disaster and accident relief.

Organizational Philosophy

Organizational Philosophy

We believe charity must uplift, not humiliate. True support enables recipients to stand on their own feet, preserving their self-respect and contributing to society as productive citizens.
