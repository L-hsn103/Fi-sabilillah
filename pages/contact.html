As detailed in Article 4 of our Constitution, temporary financial handouts frequently keep recipients trapped in recurring poverty and reliance. By providing vocational tools and productive means, beneficiaries gain lasting independence, preserving their dignity as contributing members of society.
We strictly forbid publicity photo-ops that expose beneficiaries. Aid is rendered discreetly, confidentially, and strictly for the pleasure of Allah, ensuring recipients are never subjected to public humiliation.
According to Article 13 (Clause 2), contributions from non-members must be channeled through active organization members or verified directly via our official email (fisabilillahofficial21@gmail.com). This ensures complete fiduciary accountability and prevents commercialization.
There is zero membership application fee (completely free, Article 7, Clause 3). The minimum age requirement is 16 years. Once enrolled, members contribute a voluntary baseline of 50 BDT monthly to support operations.
