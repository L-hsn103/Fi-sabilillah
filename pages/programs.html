Article 6: Operational Priorities

Our Programs

Our constitution mandates that all activities and expenditures strictly follow three prioritized programs established in Article 6.

1
Top Priority

Sustainable Self-Reliance

Helping economically disadvantaged people become self-reliant and self-respecting.

See related activities →

Detailed Explanation

We provide production tools such as sewing machines, micro-trade seed supplies, vocational instruments, and business mentoring so families generate their own livelihoods rather than relying on cyclic relief.

Objectives

  • Provide vocational tools and equipment for income generation.
  • Offer business mentoring and skills training.
  • Break the cycle of dependency on temporary relief.

Activities & Approach

  • Distribution of sewing machines and tailoring kits.
  • Micro-trade seed capital for small businesses.
  • Vocational training and mentorship.

2
Core Awareness

Ethical Giving & Human Dignity

Raising public awareness on ethical giving and the dignity of every human being.

See related activities →

Detailed Explanation

We campaign against ostentatious photo-op donations and educate society on confidential, respectful assistance that safeguards the self-esteem of beneficiaries.

Objectives

  • End publicity-driven, prestige-seeking donations.
  • Promote confidential, respectful assistance.
  • Educate donors on dignity-preserving giving.

Activities & Approach

  • Awareness campaigns on ethical giving.
  • Donor education on beneficiary dignity.
  • Community outreach against photo-op charity.

3
Emergency Standby

Disaster & Accident Relief

Rapid assistance for people affected by natural disasters or accidents.

See related activities →

Detailed Explanation

We provide rapid, empathetic assistance to communities struck by sudden natural disasters (floods, cyclones) or individual catastrophic accidents, handled with utmost confidentiality.

Objectives

  • Deliver immediate relief to disaster-affected communities.
  • Support victims of catastrophic accidents.
  • Maintain confidentiality in all relief operations.

Activities & Approach

  • Emergency ration and supply distribution.
  • Rapid response to floods and cyclones.
  • Confidential individual accident assistance.
