Leadership & Elections

Governance

Articles 9, 10, and 12 of our Constitution define our nine executive offices and an annual democratic election model built on mutual trust and accountability.

Organizational Structure

Organizational Structure

The Executive Committee comprises nine portfolios that direct all organizational operations, programs, and communications.

The Executive Committee of Fi Sabilillah comprises the following nine portfolios (as defined in Article 9 of the Constitution):

President

Provides overall organizational leadership and exercises final authority on critical decisions.

Vice President

Assists the President and assumes full responsibilities during the President's absence.

General Secretary

Supervises and coordinates all organizational operations, programs, and communications.

Organizing Secretary

Facilitates internal member coordination and manages organizational events and initiatives.

Treasurer

Manages all financial funds, records, bookkeeping, and leads the fiscal year-end audit.

Office Secretary

Preserves and archives all official records, minutes, correspondence, and documentation.

Policy Coordinator

Formulates, reviews, and refines operational policies and constitutional amendments.

Publicity Secretary

Disseminates organization news, publishes awareness materials, and handles public outreach.

Evaluation & Impact Analyst

Measures long-term outcomes of beneficiary self-reliance and conducts impact assessment.

Executive Council

Executive Council

Nine elected portfolios serve the organization through annual democratic elections held in the first week of April.

The Executive Council is elected annually through a democratic process where every active member is both a candidate and a voter. The council oversees all organizational operations and ensures accountability.

Election Process

Election Process

Every active member is both a candidate and a voter. No member may vote for themselves.

Democratic Principles (Article 12)

  • No pre-declared candidacies; all members are candidates and voters.
  • No member may vote for themselves.
  • President and General Secretary elected first, then appoint remaining officers.
  • Emergency re-election triggered if three members petition.

Election Schedule

Scheduled:

First Week of April (Annual)

Elections are held annually in the first week of April. The outgoing President heads the election commission.

Governance Rules

Governance Rules

Monthly Meetings

Regular monthly Executive Committee meetings.

General Assembly

General Assembly every three months.

Expenditure Approval

All expenditures require formal executive approval.

Annual Audit

Annual fiscal audit led by the Treasurer.

Membership Revocation

Membership may be revoked for rule violations or extended inactivity.
